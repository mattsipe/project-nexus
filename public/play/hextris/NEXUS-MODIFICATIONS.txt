Vendored:  2026-08-21, via cdn.jsdelivr.net

This is a modified version. Per GPL-3.0 §5(a), the changes are:

  1. index.html — removed the inline Google Analytics snippet
     (property UA-51272720-2).
  2. js/initialization.js — removed the inline Google Analytics snippet
     (property UA-51272720-1).
     Both sites of removal are marked with a "[Nexus]" comment.
  3. Removed CNAME (GitHub Pages domain config, meaningless when self-hosted).

Rationale: the analytics sent our visitors' data to a third party without
their consent, and the request is commonly blocked on school networks, which
stalled game load.

No game logic was altered. Complete corresponding source for this modified
version is available at the Project Nexus repository, and the unmodified upstream
remains at the URL above.
