Project Nexus — record of modifications to this vendored game
=============================================================

Upstream:  https://github.com/dmcinnes/HTML5-Asteroids
Author:    Doug McInnes
Licence:   MIT (see LICENSE, unmodified — copyright notice intact)
Vendored:  2026-08-21, via cdn.jsdelivr.net

This is a modified version. The changes are:

  1. RETITLED. Upstream calls this game "Asteroids". Nexus ships it as
     "Belt Runner". The game never draws its own title on screen and the
     upstream index.html had no <title> at all, so the whole change is one
     added <title> element plus a "[Nexus]" comment saying why, and the name
     used in the manifest. Internal identifiers (the Asteroid class, and so
     on) are code, not branding, and were left alone; so was the copyright
     header comment at the top of game.js.

     Why: "Asteroids" is a live Atari trademark. Doug McInnes's code is
     genuinely MIT and worth having, but shipping a clone under the trademark
     would claim a relationship with Atari that does not exist — and this
     project had already taken the same line on Tetris (see docs/CATALOG.md,
     docs/DECISIONS.md #21). MIT permits redistribution under a different
     name; it does not permit dropping attribution, and we have not. Doug
     McInnes is named as the author in the manifest, on the game's page, and
     in the untouched copyright notice in LICENSE.

  2. game.js — the two bare `audio.play()` calls in the SFX preload block
     now swallow their rejected promise. Browsers refuse even a *muted*
     play() before the first user gesture, so every load threw an uncaught
     NotAllowedError into the console. Guarded with a `.catch`, marked
     "[Nexus]". The preload is an optimisation; failing it changes nothing.

  3. index.html — four lines of CSS to centre the canvas in the frame.
     Upstream pinned it top-left, leaving white gutters to its right and
     below; inside the Nexus player that reads as a broken layout. Marked
     "[Nexus]".

No game logic, art or audio was otherwise altered. The game's sounds are
pre-muted upstream and are only unmuted once the player starts firing.

Audit (2026-08-21): no analytics, no third-party requests, no absolute paths.
Sound effects are triggered by collisions and firing, not on load.
