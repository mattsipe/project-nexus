Project Nexus — record of modifications to this vendored game
=============================================================

Upstream:  https://github.com/ellisonleao/clumsy-bird
Licence:   GPL-3.0 (see LICENSE.md, unmodified)
Vendored:  2026-08-21, via cdn.jsdelivr.net

This is a modified version. Per GPL-3.0 §5(a), the changes are:

  1. Removed Procfile and app.json — Heroku deployment descriptors for the
     upstream project's own hosting. Meaningless when self-hosted, and
     app.json names an upstream repository we are not deploying.

  2. index.css — `#screen` is now a full-height centring flex container.
     melonJS scales its 900x600 canvas to fit and then leaves it at the top
     of the page, so in a frame taller than 2:3 the game sat above a slab of
     flat sky. Marked "[Nexus]".

No game logic, art or audio was altered. Complete corresponding source for
this modified version is available at the Project Nexus repository, and the
unmodified upstream remains at the URL above.

Audit (2026-08-21): no analytics, no third-party requests, no absolute paths.
The theme music starts in PlayScreen, which is only reached from the title
screen after the player presses Enter or taps — nothing sounds on load.
