Project Nexus — record of modifications to this vendored game
=============================================================

Upstream:  https://github.com/jakesgordon/javascript-racer
Author:    Jake Gordon
Licence:   MIT (see LICENSE, unmodified)
Vendored:  2026-08-21, via cdn.jsdelivr.net

The repository is the companion code to Jake Gordon's write-up on pseudo-3D
road rendering, so it ships four progressively more complete versions of the
game plus a tuning harness. Nexus ships the finished game. The changes:

  1. v4.final.html is now index.html; v1/v2/v3 and the chooser page they were
     linked from are removed, along with the Rakefile.

  2. Removed music/ (7.8MB of an 8.6MB bundle). The soundtrack started on page
     load, which Nexus does not do. The <audio> element and its mute toggle go
     with it, and common.js's playMusic() returns early when the element is
     absent.

  3. index.html — the write-up's tuning panel (resolution, lane count, road
     width, camera height, draw distance, field of view, fog density) and its
     FPS meter are hidden rather than deleted: common.js reads their values
     for the starting configuration, and a display:none element still answers
     those reads. The canvas is centred on a dark ground instead of floating
     beside the panel, and the page title becomes "Racer".

All edits are marked "[Nexus]". No game logic or rendering was altered.

Audit (2026-08-21): no analytics, no third-party requests, no absolute paths,
no audio at all.
